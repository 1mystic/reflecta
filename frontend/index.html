No account needed - anonymous by design

Understanding, not recall

The same concept appears reworded. If your accuracy drops on the new phrasing, you're memorizing - and Reflecta will tell you.

Calibrated confidence

You rate your confidence on every answer. Reflecta measures how well it matches reality - and flags over- or under-confidence.

Your goal, your gaps

Tell it why you're learning. It maps your mastery onto that goal and shows the highest-leverage gap to work on next.

How it works

  1. 1
    Set your goale.g. "data science interview" - this anchors the whole analysis.
  2. 2
    Take a short quiz8–18 questions. Answer honestly and rate your confidence each time.
  3. 3
    Read your reflectionreadiness, understanding vs memorization, calibration, and what to do next.

Anonymous by design - no name, email, or IP. You can delete your responses anytime.

    How this website works

    1. 1
      Quiz engineQuestions are served with answers kept on the server; options are shuffled. Some questions are reworded twins of the same concept - that's deliberate.
    2. 2
      Behavior signalsYour choices, response times, and confidence become signals: memorization vs understanding, calibration (confidence vs reality), and answer-style profile.
    3. 3
      Mastery via IRTAn Item-Response-Theory model estimates your per-concept ability from your answers, weighted by question difficulty - fairer than raw accuracy.
    4. 4
      Intent → gapYour goal decomposes into concept requirements; your mastery is projected onto them, and the shortfall becomes your prioritized gap list.
    5. 5
      ReflectionDeterministic rules turn those numbers into specific advice - no black box between the signals and what you read.
    6. 6
      Offline researchThe knowledge-tracing models on this page (IRT, BKT, SAKT) are trained on 525k real learner interactions (ASSISTments) and tracked with MLflow.

    Privacy, in plain words

    A short, honest summary. Full notice: docs/PRIVACY.md in the repository.

    What we store - only with your consent

    • The questions shown and the option you chose
    • Whether each answer was correct
    • Your confidence rating and response time
    • The reflection we generate for you

    What we never collect

    • No name, email, phone, or account
    • No IP address stored with your session
    • Your session is an opaque random id - not linked to who you are

    What stays only in this browser

    • Your growth-tracking id and a copy of each past reflection (goal, score, and analysis) are saved in this browser's local storage, so "Reflection" in the sidebar can show your history - this data never leaves your device on its own

    Your control

    • Data is used only to generate and improve your reflection
    • Sessions auto-delete after the retention window on the server
    • You can delete a session anytime (right to erasure) - the button on your Reflection page also clears your local reflection history and growth-tracking id

    Deploying to under-age learners? Parental consent and stricter retention are required - see the full notice.
